ConceptThe Z-Transform
Definition

A power series in $z^{-1}$.

$$X(z) \,\stackrel{\text{def}}{=}\, \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x[n]\, z^{-n}$$

For finite-duration $x$, always well-defined.

For infinite-duration $x$, convergence depends on $|z|$ — that's the region of convergence.

$X(e^{j\omega})$ is the $z$-transform restricted to the unit circle — the DTFT.

ConceptENES-222 · L24
ConceptConvolution → Multiplication
Convolution theorem

$y = h * x \;\Leftrightarrow\; Y(z) = H(z)\, X(z)$

$$Y(z) \,=\, H(z)\, X(z)$$

Cascade interpretation: pass $\delta$ through $H_1$ then $H_2$. Output is $h^{(1)} * h^{(2)}$, with $z$-transform $H_1(z)\, H_2(z)$.

For FIR filters: multiply two polynomials in $z^{-1}$ — that's all.

Worked ExampleConvolution by Polynomial Multiplication
Worked example

$z$-transform shortcut.

$$b = (2, -1, 1, -2)^{\top},\;\; s = (1, 2, 3, -1)^{\top}$$

Already computed (L23) — let's recover the same answer through polynomials.

Polynomial forms
$$B(z) = 2 - z^{-1} + z^{-2} - 2 z^{-3}$$ $$S(z) = 1 + 2 z^{-1} + 3 z^{-2} - z^{-3}$$
Product
$$B(z)\, S(z) = 2 + 3 z^{-1} + 5 z^{-2} - 5 z^{-3} + 0\, z^{-4} - 7 z^{-5} + 2 z^{-6}$$ $$\Rightarrow b * s = (2, 3, 5, -5, 0, -7, 2)^{\top}\;\checkmark$$

ConceptIdeal Filters
Ideal lowpass filter

Piecewise constant
— and unbuildable.

$H(e^{j\omega}) = 1$ for $|\omega| \leq \omega_c$, $0$ otherwise.

A finite sum of cosines is smooth — no flat sections, no discontinuities. So no FIR filter (and in fact, no realisable filter) can achieve this exactly.

FIG. · Ideal lowpass — the brick wall.

ConceptA Realistic Filter Spec
What real filters look like

Pass + transition + stop.

PASSBAND
$[0,\, \omega_p]$
$|H| \in [A(1-\delta),\, A(1+\delta)]$. $\delta$ = ripple.
STOPBAND
$[\omega_s,\, \pi]$
$|H| \leq A\,\epsilon$. $1/\epsilon$ = attenuation.
TRANSITION
$[\omega_p,\, \omega_s]$
$|H|$ drops from $A(1-\delta)$ to $A\,\epsilon$.
DELAY
$M/2$ samples
Linear phase across the passband.
ConceptENES-222 · L24
ConceptSymmetric Coefficients
Symmetric $b$

$b_n = b_{M-n}$
buys linear phase + cosine sum.

$$H(e^{j\omega}) \,=\, e^{-j\omega M/2}\,F(\omega)$$

$F(\omega)$ is a real-valued sum of cosines — its sign and magnitude do all the spectral shaping.

Phase response in the passband is exactly $-M\omega/2$: uniform delay of $M/2$ samples.

Bigger $M$ ⇒ better approximation, longer delay. The trade-off.

ConceptENES-222 · L24
ConceptLowpass ⇄ Highpass
Modulation trick

Multiply $b_n$ by $(-1)^{n}$
to flip lowpass into highpass.

$$\tilde{b}_n = (-1)^{n}\, b_n$$
$$\tilde{H}(e^{j\omega}) \,=\, H(e^{j(\omega - \pi)})$$

Multiplication by $(-1)^{n}$ is modulation by the highest discrete-time frequency $\pi$ — shifts the spectrum by $\pi$ radians per sample.

A lowpass response centered at $0$ becomes a highpass response centered at $\pi$.

PracticeSquared Amplitude
Practice

Express $|H(e^{j\omega})|^{2}$
as a sum of cosines.

Filter
$$b = (1, 2)^{\top},\;\; H(z) = 1 + 2 z^{-1}$$
  • Use $|H|^{2} = H(e^{j\omega})\,H^{*}(e^{j\omega}) = H(e^{j\omega})\,H(e^{-j\omega})$.
  • Expand the product and collect.
PracticeENES-222 · L24
Solution$|H|^{2} = 5 + 4\cos\omega$
Product
$$H(e^{j\omega})\,H(e^{-j\omega}) \,=\, (1 + 2 e^{-j\omega})(1 + 2 e^{j\omega})$$
Expand
$$=\, 1 + 2 e^{j\omega} + 2 e^{-j\omega} + 4$$ $$=\, 5 + 4\cos\omega$$
Sanity check

At $\omega = 0$: $|H|^{2} = 5 + 4 = 9 = 3^{2}$. And $H(1) = 1 + 2 = 3$. $\checkmark$

At $\omega = \pi$: $|H|^{2} = 5 - 4 = 1 = 1^{2}$. And $H(-1) = 1 - 2 = -1$. $\checkmark$

$|H|^{2}$ as a finite cosine sum: always available, always real, no quadrant issues.

Solved Example A · §4.5

3-point moving average:
find $|H(e^{j\omega})|$ and zeros.

$y[n]=\frac{1}{3}(x[n]+x[n-1]+x[n-2])$.

Step 1 · System function
$$H(z)=\tfrac{1}{3}(1+z^{-1}+z^{-2})$$
Step 2 · Frequency response
$H(e^{j\omega})=\frac{e^{-j\omega}}{3}(e^{j\omega}+1+e^{-j\omega})=\frac{e^{-j\omega}(1+2\cos\omega)}{3}$ $$|H(e^{j\omega})|=\frac{|1+2\cos\omega|}{3}$$
Step 3 · Zeros
$1+2\cos\omega=0\Rightarrow\cos\omega=-1/2\Rightarrow\omega=2\pi/3,4\pi/3$

Solved Example B · §4.4.1

Cascade system function:
$H_1(z)=(1-0.5z^{-1})$, $H_2=1-0.5z^{-1}+0.25z^{-2}$.
