ENES-222 · Signals & Systems

Lecture Decks.

Twenty-four lectures spanning the complex plane, sinusoids, linear algebra, the DFT, Fourier series, and linear filtering. Click any title to open its deck.

Part I · Foundations

Complex numbers, sinusoids in continuous and discrete time, sampling, aliasing, and the foundations of linear algebra.

  1. 01 Complex Numbers The complex plane; Cartesian and polar form; coordinate conversions. §1.3
  2. 02 Geometry, Multiplication & Division Lines and circles on $\mathbb{C}$; conjugate; products and quotients in Cartesian and polar. §1.3
  3. 03 Complex Exponentials, Roots & CT Sinusoids Euler's formula; the $n$ roots of $z^n = v$; amplitude, frequency, phase. §1.3 – §1.4
  4. 04 Phasors & Discrete-Time Sinusoids Stationary phasor $Ae^{j\phi}$; DT frequency equivalence; periodicity. §1.4 – §1.5
  5. 05 Sampling Continuous-Time Sinusoids The sampling formula; $\Omega = \omega T_s$; equivalent sampling rates. §1.6
  6. 06 Aliasing $f \pm f' = k\,f_s$; reconstruction from samples; the Nyquist rate. §1.6
  7. 07 Matrix–Vector & Matrix–Matrix Products Two views of $A\mathbf{x}$; linearity; building the matrix from $A(\mathbf{e}^{(j)})$. §2.1 – §2.2.2
  8. 08 Cascaded Transformations & Permutations $AB$ as composition; $AB \neq BA$; selecting and reordering with $\mathbf{e}^{(j)}$ and $P$. §2.2.3, §2.3
  9. 09 Matrix Inversion & Gaussian Elimination Nonsingularity; triangular inverses by substitution; forward elimination + back-sub. §2.4 – §2.6

Part II · Inner Products & the DFT

Projections, complex matrices, and the full theory of the Discrete Fourier Transform — from orthogonality through structural properties, circular convolution, and sinusoid detection.

  1. 10 Inner Products, Norms & Projection Dot product; angle between vectors; orthogonal projection onto a subspace. §2.10
  2. 11 Complex Matrices & Vectors Hermitian transpose $V^H$; complex inner product; $V^HV = NI$ for the Fourier matrix. §2.13
  3. 12 Introduction to the DFT Analysis and synthesis equations; the DFT as a change of basis; $N$-point examples. §3.1 – §3.2
  4. 13 DFT of Real Signals Conjugate symmetry $S[N-k]=S[k]^*$; amplitude spectrum; reading a spectrum. §3.3.1
  5. 14 Circular Shift & Reversal Permutation matrix $P$; reversal matrix $R$; DFT-shift and DFT-reversal properties. §3.2.2, §3.3.2, §3.4
  6. 15 Shift, Modulation & Duality Modulation matrix $F^k$; DFT-modulation; duality $S \leftrightarrow NR\mathbf{x}$. §3.4
  7. 16 Circular Convolution Element-wise product $\leftrightarrow$ circular convolution; DFT-based computation. §3.6
  8. 17 Zero-Padding & Detecting Sinusoids Zero-padded extension; spectral interpolation; locating a sinusoid from its DFT peak. §3.7 – §3.8

Periodic signals in continuous time, FIR filter design, linear convolution, and the z-transform connecting the time and frequency domains.

  1. 18 Fourier Series Introduction Synthesis and analysis equations; FS coefficients of common periodic signals. §FS
  2. 19 Fourier Series Coefficients Computing $X_k$ by integration; time-shift property; Parseval's theorem. §FS
  3. 20 Fourier Series Properties Linearity, time shift, conjugate symmetry; even/odd signal decomposition. §FS
  4. 21 FIR Filters & Frequency Response Input–output relation; system function $H(z)$; frequency response $H(e^{j\Omega})$; amplitude and phase. §4.3 – §4.4.1
  5. 22 FIR Filters for Periodic Inputs Response to sinusoidal and exponential inputs; cascaded filters; circular convolution via DFT. §4.4.2, §4.4.4 – §4.4.5
  6. 23 Linear Convolution Convolution sum; polynomial multiplication; linear vs. circular; block convolution. §4.6 – §4.7
  7. 24 Z-Transform & Frequency-Selective Filters $Y(z)=H(z)X(z)$; ideal and practical FIR filters; amplitude response and filter design. §4.5 – §4.6.5
